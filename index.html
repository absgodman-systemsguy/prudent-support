Household spending, together.

Common questions

How do I share with my partner? Settings → Sharing & sync → Invite someone, then send the invite with Messages or Mail, or choose Copy Link and paste it into a chat. They open the link on their iPhone and join. Both of you need to be signed in to iCloud.

Do I need iCloud? No. Without iCloud, Prudent works fully on your iPhone. iCloud adds backup and sharing.

Where is my data? On your iPhone and in your own iCloud. The developer can't see it. See the privacy policy.

Can I get my expenses out? Yes: Settings → Your data → Export all expenses (CSV). You can import a CSV in the same format too.

How do I delete everything? Settings → Privacy & security → Delete all my data. It explains exactly what will be deleted and asks you to confirm.

Can I lock the app? Yes: Settings → Privacy & security → App Lock uses Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode.
