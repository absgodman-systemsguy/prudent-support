Prudent Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

Prudent helps a household keep track of what it spends. This policy explains what happens to the information you put into it.

What Prudent stores

  • The expenses you add: amount, category, description, date, who paid, and whether it's a fixed monthly cost.
  • Your household's name, the names you give yourselves, the currency and your budgets.
  • A few settings on your iPhone (the look you chose, App Lock, and which household member you are).

Prudent never asks for bank logins, card numbers or passwords.

Where it's kept

Your data is saved on your iPhone and synced through your own iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit. Households you create are kept in your iCloud; households you join are kept in the iCloud of the person who invited you. The developer of Prudent can't see or access any of it.

If you're not signed in to iCloud, your data stays on your iPhone only.

Sharing with your household

When you invite someone, Apple's iCloud sharing gives them access to that household's expenses, budgets and member names, and they can add and change expenses. Only the people you invite (or anyone with the link, if you choose that option) can join. You can remove people or stop sharing at any time in Settings → Sharing & sync → Manage people.

Receipt photos and pasted messages

Photos and pasted text are read on your iPhone to find amounts. They aren't saved and aren't sent anywhere. Only the expenses you choose to add are kept.

What Prudent doesn't do

  • No analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • No third-party services or SDKs.
  • Nothing is sold or shared with anyone.

Deleting your data

Settings → Privacy & security → Delete all my data deletes your households and expenses from your iPhone and from iCloud. If you joined someone else's household, it removes your name and takes you out of it; the expenses you added stay with that household, shown as "Former member". Deleting the app removes the copy on that iPhone; your data stays in iCloud until you use Delete all my data.

You can also export all your expenses as a CSV file at any time (Settings → Your data).

Children

Prudent isn't directed at children.
